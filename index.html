FAQ

Questions business owners ask

Do I need to install anything?

No. Your office lives in a private WhatsApp group on the phone you already use, and the dashboard opens in any browser. We set it up in a day.

Does it use my prices and my logo?

Yes. We load your price list and brand once; every quote, invoice and receipt goes out in your name with your terms.

Can it spend money or message my clients on its own?

No. The agent never spends money, never sends a bulk message and never sends a client a new document without your word. Routine follow-ups run on a schedule you approved.

What if it gets something wrong?

Every document is previewed to you before it reaches a client, and a person at AiTask reviews each office daily. Mistakes are corrected and the fix is built into the agent so it doesn't repeat.

Where is my data?

In your own account, on a Canadian-region cloud, backed up daily. It stays yours — export it any time.

Is the demo real?

The demo runs the real dashboard with fictional clients and numbers, so you can click anything. Your office will look the same with your data.

Who is behind it?

AiTask is a Toronto company. The system was built to run a real Toronto contracting business first, then packaged for others.
