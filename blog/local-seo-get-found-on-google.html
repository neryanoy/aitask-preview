Local SEO: How Small Businesses Get Found on Google

When someone searches for your service nearby, do they find you — or a competitor? Local SEO decides the answer.

What is local SEO?

Local SEO is the work that helps your business appear when people search for services "near me" or in your city. It combines your website, your Google Business Profile, and signals across the web that tell Google you are a trusted local choice.

The essentials

  • Google Business Profile — claim and complete it; it powers the map results and is one of the strongest local signals.
  • Consistent NAP — your name, address and phone should match everywhere online.
  • Reviews — genuine customer reviews build trust and rankings.
  • On-page SEO — clear titles, descriptions and content that mention your services and area.
  • Helpful content — pages and articles that answer what local customers search for.

It builds over time

Local SEO is not instant. It compounds as your profile, content and reviews grow. The businesses that show up first are usually the ones that started and stayed consistent.
